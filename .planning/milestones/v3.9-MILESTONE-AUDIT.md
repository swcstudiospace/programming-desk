## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v3.9 extends Programming Desk with post-quantum cryptographic primitives, lattice-based attestation, and quantum-resistant audit ledgers across federated desks.
Key exchanges utilize hybrid KEM combining classical X25519 with NIST ML-KEM-768 (`HybridKEM`), and message authentication is secured using hybrid signatures combining Ed25519 with NIST ML-DSA-65 (`HybridSignatureEngine`).
Duplex inter-desk channels feature replay protection with monotonic counters (`PQCChannelSession`), and active cipher negotiation is continuously audited to intercept downgrade attacks (`QuantumAuditInspector`).
The ledger layer adopts SHA3-256 state leaves with lattice root checkpointing (`PQCMerkleLedger`), seat identities are certified by a lattice-attested Certificate Authority (`PQCIdentityAuthority`), remote seat validity is verified across desks (`CrossDeskLatticeVerifier`), and immutable commitments are exported to Solana devnet anchors (`PQCAnchorExporter`).
Resilience is validated through end-to-end drill simulation (`QuantumAttackDrillSimulator`).

All 10 requirements across Phase 44 and Phase 45 are fully implemented and verified:
- **Phase 44 Requirements (`REQ-PQC-001` through `REQ-PQC-005`)**: 100% verified.
- **Phase 45 Requirements (`REQ-PQC-006` through `REQ-PQC-010`)**: 100% verified.
