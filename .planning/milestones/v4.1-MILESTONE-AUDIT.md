## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v4.1 equips Programming Desk with decentralized governance and algorithmic compute credit settlement across federated desks.
Governance proposals follow a deterministic state machine (`SwarmDAOEngine`), utilizing quadratic voting weight based on effective stake and reputation (`StakeReputationRegistry`), protected by timelocks (`PolicyTimelockExecutor`).
Resource settlement operates via an algorithmic compute credit ledger (`ComputeCreditLedger`), pricing compute dynamic to congestion, atomic clearinghouse batch coordination (`CrossDeskClearinghouse`), off-chain payment channels (`PaymentChannelManager`), and Solana devnet attestation proofs (`SettlementAnchorExporter`).
Solvency and attack resilience are verified through `TokenomicsDrillSimulator`.

All 10 requirements across Phase 48 and Phase 49 are fully implemented and verified:
- **Phase 48 Requirements (`REQ-DAO-001` through `REQ-DAO-005`)**: 100% verified.
- **Phase 49 Requirements (`REQ-DAO-006` through `REQ-DAO-010`)**: 100% verified.
