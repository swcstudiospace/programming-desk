## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v4.0 extends Programming Desk with distributed cross-desk workflow orchestration, task DAG compilation, resource preemption scheduling, federated capability brokers, execution receipts, and automatic failure recovery DAG synthesis.
Workflows are compiled into dependency DAGs (`CrossDeskWorkflowCompiler`), dynamically executed across seats with typed context forwarding (`WorkflowExecutionEngine`, `DependencyPipeline`), and balanced with preemption (`SwarmResourceScheduler`).
Remote tool capabilities are federated across desks (`CapabilityFederationBroker`), immutable execution receipts are signed and committed to Merkle roots (`WorkflowReceiptLedger`), and exported to Solana devnet anchors (`WorkflowAnchorExporter`).
Node or capability failures trigger automatic fallback synthesis (`WorkflowFailureSynthesizer`), verified end-to-end via `SwarmOrchestrationDrillSimulator`.

All 10 requirements across Phase 46 and Phase 47 are fully implemented and verified:
- **Phase 46 Requirements (`REQ-SWARM-001` through `REQ-SWARM-005`)**: 100% verified.
- **Phase 47 Requirements (`REQ-SWARM-006` through `REQ-SWARM-010`)**: 100% verified.
