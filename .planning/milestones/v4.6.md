# Milestone v4.6 — Autonomous Self-Reflective Metacognition & Continuous Epistemic Verification Mesh

**Target Version:** `v4.6.0`
**Timeline:** October 2026
**Branch:** `feat/milestone-v4.6-phase58-phase59-metacognition-epistemic-mesh`
**Scope:** Phases 58 & 59

## Executive Summary

Milestone v4.6 endows the Programming Desk multi-agent autonomous swarm with self-reflective metacognition and continuous epistemic verification. Rather than passively accepting model completions and confidence outputs, agents dynamically calibrate epistemic uncertainty (Brier score & Expected Calibration Error), introspect reasoning chains for cognitive biases and circularity, dynamically toggle System 1/System 2 deliberative strategy policies, synthesize adversarial counter-evidence Socratic challenges, enforce cross-desk epistemic consistency, and anchor cryptographic epistemic commitments to Solana devnet.

---

## Phase Breakdown

### Phase 58: Autonomous Epistemic Calibration & Self-Reflective Metacognition
- **Epistemic Confidence Calibrator (`EpistemicCalibrator`, `CalibratedConfidence`, `ConfidenceBucket`)**: Calibrates raw agent confidence scores using empirical Platt scaling / temperature scaling, computing Brier scores and Expected Calibration Error (ECE) across confidence bins.
- **Metacognitive Introspection Engine (`MetacognitiveIntrospector`, `SelfReflectionTrace`, `CognitiveBiasReport`)**: Synthesizes introspective reflection traces, diagnosing circular reasoning, confirmation bias, overconfidence spikes, and premature termination.
- **Epistemic Belief Network (`EpistemicBeliefNetwork`, `BeliefNode`, `BeliefUpdate`)**: Bayesian evidence assimilation engine tracking discrete hypothesis priors, updating posteriors via log-likelihood ratios, and partitioning uncertainty into aleatoric (data noise) and epistemic (knowledge deficit) components.
- **Introspective Strategy Optimizer (`IntrospectiveStrategyOptimizer`)**: Metacognitive meta-policy selecting execution strategy (`FAST_HEURISTIC`, `DELIBERATE_VERIFICATION`, `ADVERSARIAL_DEBATE`, `DEEP_CONSENSUS`) based on epistemic entropy and task criticality.
- **REST API routes under `/v1/metacognition/*`**: Exposing calibration, introspection analysis, belief network updates, and strategy optimization.

### Phase 59: Continuous Epistemic Verification, Counter-Evidence Synthesis & Epistemic Audit Mesh
- **Automated Counter-Evidence Synthesizer (`CounterEvidenceSynthesizer`, `SocraticChallenge`)**: Proactively synthesizes counter-hypotheses, falsification edge cases, and devil's advocate challenges to stress-test consensus hypotheses.
- **Epistemic Consistency Verifier (`EpistemicConsistencyVerifier`)**: Measures cross-desk belief alignment using symmetric Kullback-Leibler and Jensen-Shannon divergence, flagging epistemic drift or ideological polarization.
- **Epistemic Proof Receipt Ledger (`EpistemicReceiptLedger`, `EpistemicProofReceipt`)**: Immutable HMAC-SHA256 receipt log storing calibration updates, belief transitions, and challenge refutations.
- **External Epistemic Anchor & Solana Devnet Exporter (`EpistemicAnchorExporter`)**: Commits Merkle roots of epistemic proof receipts to Solana devnet targets with transaction receipts.
- **End-to-End Metacognitive & Epistemic Verification Drill Simulator (`MetacognitiveEpistemicDrillSimulator`)**: Multi-point resilience drill validating confidence calibration, bias introspection, Socratic falsification challenge, cross-desk Jensen-Shannon coherence, and Solana devnet anchoring.
- **REST API routes under `/v1/epistemic/*` and `/v1/metacognition/drill/simulate`**: Exposing Socratic challenges, consistency verifications, receipt ledger audits, and drill execution.
