# Milestone v3.9: Post-Quantum Cryptographic Migration & Lattice-Based Attestation Mesh

## Overview

Milestone v3.9 establishes post-quantum cryptographic primitives, hybrid key encapsulation, lattice digital signatures, and quantum-resistant audit ledgers across federated Programming Desks.
Phase 44 delivers hybrid KEM (classical X25519 combined with NIST ML-KEM-768 / Kyber), hybrid digital signatures (Ed25519 combined with ML-DSA-65 / Dilithium), encrypted duplex channel sessions with replay counters, and active quantum audit inspection detecting cipher downgrade attempts.
Phase 45 provides quantum-resistant SHA3-256 Merkle audit ledgers, lattice-attested seat identity passports, cross-desk lattice attestation verification, Solana devnet quantum commitment anchors, and comprehensive quantum attack and downgrade drill simulators.
