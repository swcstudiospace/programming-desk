# Roadmap: Milestone v2.2 — Multi-Desk Federation & Staging Deployments

## Overview

Milestone **v2.2 — Multi-Desk Federation & Staging Deployments** enables inter-desk federation, cross-instance task graph synchronization, partition-tolerant fail-open routing, declarative VPS staging deployment with automated rollback, zero-downtime hot reloading, ephemeral lease reconciliation, and cross-repo companion contract verification.
