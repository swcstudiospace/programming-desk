## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v3.8 extends Programming Desk with dynamic partition sharding, multi-master geo-replication, and sovereign mesh consensus across geographically distributed desks.
Nodes are partitioned via a consistent hash ring with virtual vnodes (`ConsistentHashRing`), and cross-region state mutations are coordinated asynchronously using Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types (`CRDTStore` supporting LWW-Register, PN-Counter, and OR-Set) alongside vector-clocked HMAC-SHA256 replication deltas (`GeoReplicationEngine`).
WAN partitions are monitored via vector-clock anti-entropy gossip (`AntiEntropyGossip`), split-brain conditions are strictly fenced using dynamic majority quorums (`SplitBrainDetector`), and dual-primary writes are prevented through monotonic leases (`EpochCoordinator`).
Upon WAN re-establishment, divergent deltas are reconciled and resynchronized automatically (`PartitionHealingOrchestrator`), as proven by the end-to-end multi-region partition and healing drill simulator (`GeoPartitionDrillSimulator`).

All 10 requirements across Phase 42 and Phase 43 are fully implemented and verified:
- **Phase 42 Requirements (`REQ-SHARD-001` through `REQ-SHARD-005`)**: 100% verified.
- **Phase 43 Requirements (`REQ-SHARD-006` through `REQ-SHARD-010`)**: 100% verified.
